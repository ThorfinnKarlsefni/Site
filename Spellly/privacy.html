Privacy Policy

Learning stays personal.

Effective and last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

Spellly is an English spelling practice app for iPhone and iPad. The app does not require an account, does not include advertising, and does not track you across apps or websites. The developer does not sell personal information.

Learning data stored on your device

Spellly stores practice history, review words, chapter progress, free-use progress, preferences, and cached subscription status locally on your device. This learning data is not uploaded to a server operated by the developer.

Handwriting recognition and Google ML Kit

On supported practice screens, handwriting strokes are recognized on your device using Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The handwriting input and recognition result are processed on-device and are not sent to Google. ML Kit may connect to Google to download or update recognition models and related components. It also sends limited SDK performance and usage metrics to Google, such as device and app information, a per-installation identifier, latency, API configuration, and the configured recognition language. Google uses this information for diagnostics, usage analytics, maintenance, improvement, and abuse prevention.

Speech and keyboard

English pronunciation uses Apple system speech services. Text and handwriting input use Apple’s keyboard and input frameworks. Their operation is governed by your device settings and Apple’s applicable privacy terms. Spellly does not receive personal account information from these system services.

Subscriptions and purchases

Spellly Pro subscriptions are offered through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payments and manages subscription transactions. The developer does not receive your full payment-card information. The app reads verified transaction status to unlock subscription features.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Spellly does not include advertising SDKs, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not use the developer’s own analytics service. As described above, the integrated Google ML Kit SDK collects limited diagnostics and usage metrics. These metrics are not used by Spellly for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data retention and deletion

Learning data remains on your device until it is removed by the app or you delete the app. Deleting Spellly removes its locally stored learning data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior. Subscription records are retained by Apple under Apple’s policies.

Children

Spellly does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The app does not require an account or ask users to submit a name, email address, or precise location. If you believe there is a privacy issue involving a child, contact the developer.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Spellly’s features or integrated services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.
